# Chapter 2: Digitization of Information

> **Algorithm Goal:** Extract hidden discrete patterns from spectral data, build an energy quantization model, and verify it through hardware-level experiments.

**Corresponding topics: Blackbody radiation, spectra, Bohr model, Franck-Hertz experiment**

---

The previous chapter ended with a Bug Report: the "analog circuit logic" of classical physics cannot sustain a stable atom. The solution points in a radical direction, **discretization**.

But this idea did not appear out of thin air. Before Rutherford scattering, physicists had already accumulated a vast amount of data on atomic emission, **spectra**. These data were like encrypted signals sent by atoms, waiting to be decoded.

This chapter tells three stories:

1. **Data Mining**: How Balmer pieced together an empirical formula from spectral data (a human-powered version of symbolic regression).
2. **Forced Quantization**: How Bohr "pixelated" continuous orbits by introducing an angular momentum quantization condition.
3. **Hardware Verification**: How the Franck-Hertz experiment proved in the most direct way that energy levels are discrete.
