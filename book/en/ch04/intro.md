# Chapter 4: The Ultimate Algorithm

> **Algorithm Goal:** Master the core "engine" of quantum mechanics, the Schrodinger equation, understand the role of operators as information extraction tools, and solve the complete wavefunction of the hydrogen atom.

**Corresponding content: Schrodinger equation, expectation values and operators, solution of the hydrogen atom, orbital magnetic moment**

---

The first three chapters established the conceptual foundations of quantum mechanics:

- Chapter 1: The atom has a nuclear structure, but classical models are unstable.
- Chapter 2: Energy is quantized (discrete).
- Chapter 3: The state of a particle is described by the wavefunction $\psi$, and $|\psi|^2$ gives the probability density.

But we are still missing the most crucial piece: **where does $\psi$ itself come from?** What equation does it satisfy? How can we start from this equation to derive specific energy levels and wavefunctions?

This chapter answers that question. The Schrodinger equation is the core algorithm of quantum mechanics, just as Newton's second law $F=ma$ is the core algorithm of classical mechanics. Given the potential energy function (Hamiltonian) of a system, the Schrodinger equation outputs all allowed quantum states.
